Rationale (the stages of the argument): 1, the question and the paradox (a people that could be free by ceasing to
serve); 2, liberty is natural (nature, the animals, three kinds of tyrants); 3, first cause: custom (Syracuse,
Lycurgus, the Spartans and Xerxes, Cato); 4, second cause: tyrants make their subjects cowardly and dull (games,
largesse, religion, the French emblems); 5, the « ressort et secret de la domination »: the chain of accomplices and
the misery of the favourites; 6, the tyrant can have no friend, and conclusion.
