# Le Dernier Jour d'un condamné, Victor Hugo: source

- Work: Victor Hugo, *Le Dernier Jour d'un condamné*, first published 7 February 1829 (Paris, Charles Gosselin, without
  the author's name). Author died in 1885: public domain.
- Transcription: French Wikisource, https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Le_Dernier_Jour_d%E2%80%99un_Condamn%C3%A9 (text quality:
  « Textes validés »), one page per chapter, `Le Dernier Jour d'un Condamné/01` to `/49`, from the index
  `Livre:Hugo - Œuvres complètes, Impr. nat., Roman, tome I.djvu`, starting at page 623 of the printed volume.
- Edition transcribed: *Œuvres complètes de Victor Hugo*, édition dite de l'Imprimerie nationale, Roman, tome I,
  Paris, Imprimerie nationale / Ollendorff, 1910 (text established by Gustave Simon). Same edition as `claude-gueux`.
- Licence of the transcription: Wikisource, CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Retrieved 2026-10-17 with `index.php?action=render` (throttled), HTML stripped to paragraphs.
- Cleaning: Wikisource header/navigation, page-number anchors and footnote markers removed. Paragraphs are separated by
  a blank line; dialogue lines keep their dash. Each file starts with the chapter numeral as printed (« I », « II »…);
  `01.txt` also keeps the title « LE DERNIER JOUR D'UN CONDAMNÉ ». The argot song of chapter XVI keeps its verse
  lines (one line per verse, a blank line where the printed page breaks the song).
- Footnotes: only chapter XXIII has notes in this edition, 23 short glosses of the old convict's argot (e.g. « le taule »:
  « Le bourreau » ; « la placarde »: « Place de Grève » ; « ratichon »: « Le prêtre »). They are not in `23.txt`.
- Not included as units (they are prefaces, not part of the 49 chapters): the « Préface » dated 15 March 1832 (first
  printed in the 5th edition, Renduel, 1832), « Une comédie à propos d'une tragédie » (3rd edition, 28 February 1829),
  and the « Note » printed after chapter XLIX (presentation of the facsimile of the argot song). Dates from the
  editor's notes of the 1910 edition (« Historique » and « Notice bibliographique », same Wikisource text).
- Chapter XLVII (« MON HISTOIRE ») contains only the fictional « Note de l'éditeur »: it is part of the novel and is
  kept as a unit.
