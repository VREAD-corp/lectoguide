# Claude Gueux, Victor Hugo: source

- Work: Victor Hugo, *Claude Gueux*, first published in 1834. Author died in 1885: public domain.
- Transcription: French Wikisource, https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Claude_Gueux (text quality: « Textes validés »),
  pages 763 to 782 of the index `Livre:Hugo - Œuvres complètes, Impr. nat., Roman, tome I.djvu`.
- Edition transcribed: *Œuvres complètes de Victor Hugo*, édition dite de l'Imprimerie nationale, Roman, tome I,
  Paris, Imprimerie nationale / Ollendorff, 1910 (editor Gustave Simon).
- Licence of the transcription: Wikisource, CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Retrieved 2026-10-16 with the MediaWiki API (`action=parse&prop=text`), HTML stripped to paragraphs.
- Cleaning: Wikisource header/navigation, page-number anchors and the two footnote markers removed. Paragraphs are
  separated by a blank line; dialogue lines keep their dash. The title « CLAUDE GUEUX » is kept at the top of
  `01.txt` and is not counted as a paragraph.
- The two footnotes are the author's own notes; they are not in the unit files, and are given here in full:
  1. after « je l'ai jugé et je l'ai condamné à mort » (Claude's speech to the detainees, § 120, file `03.txt`):
     « Textuel. »
  2. after « sous le bon plaisir de mon épicier, dont on a fait mon officier » (§ 227, file `05.txt`): « Il va sans
     dire que nous n'entendons pas attaquer ici la patrouille urbaine, chose utile, qui garde la rue, le seuil et le
     foyer ; mais seulement la parade, le pompon, la gloriole et le tapage militaire, choses ridicules, qui ne servent
     qu'à faire du bourgeois une parodie du soldat. »

## Units

The text has no chapters. It is cut into five parts by paragraph ranges, numbered § 1 to § 244 from the first
paragraph after the title (every blank-line-separated block counts as one paragraph, short dialogue lines included):

Total: 8503 words (whitespace-separated tokens, title included).

- Partie 1 (§ 1 à 32): Claude, his theft and sentence, Clairvaux, the workshop director, hunger, friendship with Albin.
- Partie 2 (§ 33 à 93): the director separates Albin from Claude; refusals; the ultimatum of 25 October to 4 November.
- Partie 3 (§ 94 à 172): 4 November, the hatchet, the speech to the detainees, the murder, the suicide attempt.
- Partie 4 (§ 173 à 217): recovery, trial at Troyes, sentence, appeal, execution.
- Partie 5 (§ 218 à 244): the narrator's plea to the deputies (education, penal reform, death penalty).
